- `public/assets/office.png`: 1536×1024. Prompt: “Wide orthographic overhead 16-bit pixel RPG office-house floor plan; no people, UI, words or logos. Upper-left private studio, upper-middle meeting room with a large table and slate-blue tiles, upper-right library. Connected corridor, lower communal workshop/lounge with desks, sofas and indoor garden, entrance at bottom center. Cutaway cream walls, warm wood, forest green and muted orange. Scene fills the image.”
- `public/assets/residents.png`: 1536×1024 RGBA, 3×2 equal cells. Prompt: “Six friendly 16-bit RPG professionals, full bodies facing forward, consistent scale/alignment, genuine transparent background, no labels, grid or text. Woman in teal jacket; white robot with teal face; man in navy; orange robot; woman in purple cardigan; violet robot. Crisp chibi silhouettes, ample cell padding.” A transparency refinement preserved designs and removed residual backdrop.
